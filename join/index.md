## Opportunities

Our group is always interested in hearing from excellent and motivated students and postdoctoral researchers. We typically welcome approximately **1–2 new group members per year**. If you think your research interests and background may be a good fit, please feel free to reach out.

For additional information about research interests, expectations, and potential opportunities, please  our **[group information PDF](https://tanyenjoe.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/fit-1.pdf)**.

Below is a non-exhaustive list of useful resources for prospective applicants.

### Prospective Graduate Students

* **[CUHK Student Summer Workshop for Potential PhD Applicants](https://www.ees.cuhk.edu.hk/programme/postgraduate-programmes/postgraduate-programme/leadership-camp/)**  
  **13–17 July 2026**  
  Application deadline: **24 May 2026**

* **[CUHK MPhil and PhD Program](https://www.ees.cuhk.edu.hk/programme/postgraduate-programmes/postgraduate-programme/)**  
  The regular application deadline is typically around the **end of January** each year.  
  Admitted students receive a monthly stipend of approximately **USD $2,400** and may apply for on-campus student housing, which typically costs approximately **USD $400 per month**.

* **[Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme](https://cerg1.ugc.edu.hk/hkpfs/index.html)**  
  Application deadline: typically around **1 December** each year.  
  Fellowship recipients receive a monthly stipend of approximately **USD $3,600**, an annual conference-travel allowance of approximately **USD $1,800**, a one-time housing allowance of approximately **USD $5,000**, and access to on-campus student housing.

### Prospective Postdoctoral Fellows

* **[RGC Postdoctoral Fellowship Scheme](https://www.ugc.edu.hk/eng/rgc/funding_opport/jrfs/index.html)**  
  Internal nomination deadline: typically around **early September** each year.  
  Fellowship recipients receive an annual stipend of approximately **USD $55,000** for **three years**.

* **[香江学者计划 / Xiangjiang Scholars Program](https://www.hkscholars.org/a-list/10545-eng)**  
  Application deadline: typically around **early January** each year.  
  Fellowship recipients receive an annual stipend of approximately **USD $47,000** for **two years**.

## What We Value

We are looking for researchers who demonstrate:

* **Deep curiosity** about geophysics, geohazards, earthquakes, and Earth processes.
* **Commitment to growth** through continuous learning, constructive feedback, and technical development.
* **Integrity and intellectual honesty** in data analysis, scientific communication, and research practice.
* **Determination to make original contributions** through focus, creativity, resilience, and resourcefulness.
* **Respectful collaboration** with colleagues, mentors, students, and the broader scientific community.

## What We Aim to Offer

Members of the group can expect:

* Scientific, professional, and personal guidance aligned with their career and life goals.
* A supportive environment where researchers are treated fairly and with respect.
* Opportunities to develop computational, analytical, and scientific communication skills.
* Freedom and encouragement to explore meaningful research questions.
* Mentorship that can continue beyond a specific project, degree, or appointment.
