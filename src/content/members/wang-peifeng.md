## Research

I am a geophysicist working in seismology and submarine volcanism. My research interests include applying seismic observations and waveform-based methods to investigate Earth structure, seismic sources, and submarine eruptive processes.

I am particularly interested in using advanced seismic monitoring techniques—including distributed acoustic sensing, noise interferometry, waveform modeling, and seismic imaging—to better understand geological processes in both terrestrial and marine environments.

## Background

I received an M.S. degree from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2022.

## Research interests

- Seismology  
- Submarine eruptions and volcanism  
- Seismic monitoring  
- Distributed acoustic sensing (DAS)  
- Seismic interferometry  
- Waveform modeling and mantle discontinuities  
- Marine seismic imaging
