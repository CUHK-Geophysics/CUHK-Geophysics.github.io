# Welcome to the Geophysics Laboratory @ CUHK!
{: .display-4}
<br>
We are part of the [Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences](https://www.ees.cuhk.edu.hk/), at the [The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/).
{: .welcomefont}


Our mission is to learn as much as possible about earthquake, volcanic, and earth surface processes while enjoying the journey along the way. Our ultimate hope is to learn things that can help mitigate these potential hazards and contribute towards sustainable development of society.
{: .welcomefont}

We are located in Mong Man Wai Building at main Campus of CUHK.
{: .welcomefont}
